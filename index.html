Privacy Policy - LifeTap

Application: LifeTap  |  Package ID: com.ymstudio.lifetap
Developer: YMStudio  |  Effective Date: September 18, 2026

1. Overview

LifeTap ("we", "our", or "the application") is a single-tap daily activity logger and personal pattern analysis tool developed by YMStudio.

LifeTap is built strictly around a 100% Offline and Local-first architecture. We believe your personal habits, routines, and logs belong solely to you. This Privacy Policy details why and how we do not collect, process, or transmit any of your personal data.

2. Information Collection and Storage

a. User-Generated Activity Data

  • What you record: Activity categories (such as drinking water, meals, workouts, sleep, medication), timestamps, optional notes, and custom event categories you create.
  • Where it is stored: All data is stored exclusively in a local SQLite database (life_logger.db) on your physical device, within the application's secure operating system sandbox.
  • Zero External Transmission: Your logs, timestamps, notes, and preferences are never uploaded to external servers, cloud storage, or third parties. The application functions completely without internet connectivity.

b. Personally Identifiable Information (PII)

LifeTap does not require accounts, registration, social logins, or sign-in credentials. We do not collect or request your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or financial details.

c. Analytics, Telemetry, and Advertising

  • LifeTap contains zero third-party analytics SDKs (no Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or Mixpanel).
  • LifeTap contains zero advertising SDKs and does not track Advertising Identifiers (Google Advertising ID / AAID or Apple IDFA).
  • No background tracking or diagnostic telemetry is ever transmitted from your device.

3. Device Permissions

LifeTap does not request any sensitive or dangerous device permissions:

  • No Location Access: Does not access GPS or network location.
  • No Camera or Microphone Access: Does not capture photos, video, or audio.
  • No Contacts or Calendar Access: Does not read address books or external calendars.
  • No Dangerous Storage Permissions: Data resides strictly in the app’s internal sandbox.

The application only utilizes standard vibration (HapticFeedback) to provide subtle tactile feedback when tapping event cards.

4. User Rights and Data Deletion

Because all data is stored exclusively on your device, you maintain full control at all times:

  • Delete Individual Entries: Swipe left on any entry in the "Timeline" tab to delete it.
  • Delete Custom Categories: Delete user-created event types in the "Manage Event Types" sheet.
  • Erase All Data: Permanently erase all records simply by clearing the application storage via your device’s Settings > Apps > LifeTap > Storage > Clear Storage, or by uninstalling the application.

5. Children's Privacy

LifeTap does not collect personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13. It is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Google Play Families Policy.
